No. 01

Financial Performance Dashboard from Tally Exports

Advanced Excel · SQL · Financial reporting

Problem statement

A small trading firm exported its books from Tally Prime as flat CSVs every month but had no consolidated view of margin, expense ratio, or cash position across periods — every review meant rebuilding the same pivot table by hand.

Approach

Exported ledgers were loaded into SQL Server, normalised into a fact table by voucher type, and joined against a chart-of-accounts dimension. Advanced Excel — Power Query, INDEX/MATCH, and dynamic pivot dashboards — turned that model into a monthly management pack.

Tech stack

  • SQL (data modelling)
  • Power Query
  • Advanced Excel (pivots, INDEX/MATCH, what-if analysis)
  • Tally Prime exports

Key metrics analysed

  • Gross & net profit margin
  • Operating expense ratio
  • Accounts receivable ageing
  • Month-over-month cash position

Visual layout recommendation

Single-page Excel dashboard: KPI strip across the top (margin, expenses, cash), a trend line for revenue vs. expense beneath it, and an ageing table for receivables in the lower-left, with a period slicer controlling every element at once.

No. 02

Retail Sales & Profitability Dashboard

Power BI · SQL · Business intelligence

Problem statement

Regional managers at a multi-store retailer could see total sales but not which stores, categories, or promotions were actually profitable — decisions on discounting and stock allocation were being made on revenue alone.

Interactive features

Store-level drill-through from a regional map, a date-range slicer synced across every visual, and a profitability toggle that recolours the entire report by margin instead of revenue on click.

Tech stack

  • SQL (staging & aggregation)
  • Power BI (DAX measures)
  • Power Query (transformation)

Key KPIs displayed

  • Revenue vs. target by region
  • Gross margin % by category
  • Basket size & units per transaction
  • Promotion lift vs. baseline sales

Business impact

Surfacing margin alongside revenue reframed which categories were worth promoting, and gave regional managers a single source of truth to defend stock and pricing decisions in weekly reviews.

No. 03

Customer Churn Analysis & Revenue Trend Study

Python · Pandas · Seaborn · SQL

Problem statement

A subscription business needed to know which customer segments were driving churn and how that churn was likely to affect revenue over the next two quarters, using twelve months of transaction and support-ticket history.

Data cleaning steps

  • Merged billing, usage, and support-ticket tables via SQL joins
  • Handled missing plan-tier values with rule-based imputation
  • Standardised inconsistent date and currency formats
  • Flagged and removed duplicate billing events

Analysis & insights

Exploratory analysis in Pandas and Seaborn showed churn concentrated in a specific plan tier within the first 90 days, correlated with support-ticket volume rather than price. A simple trend model projected the resulting revenue impact if the pattern continued unaddressed.

Repository highlights

  • Jupyter notebook with a documented cleaning pipeline
  • Reusable churn cohort & retention charts
  • Written summary of findings and recommendations
